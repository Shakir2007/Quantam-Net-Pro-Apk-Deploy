Why QuantamNetPro Mobile?

Unlike bloated commercial speed tests stuffed with ads and tracking scripts, QuantamNetPro is engineered for raw surgical precision and authentic network engineering diagnostics.

RFC 3550 Jitter Telemetry

Precision statistical measurement of packet arrival variance down to the microsecond level. Essential for voice, gaming, and real-time streaming reliability.

Bufferbloat Stress Testing

Measures latency degradation while fully saturating upstream and downstream buffers, assigning an authentic Bufferbloat Grade (A+ to F).

Zero Bloat & Zero Ads

No background tracking, no advertising SDKs, and no hidden battery drain. 100% of CPU cycles are dedicated to diagnostic accuracy.

Global BGP Anycast Nodes

Connects directly to low-latency edge points-of-presence worldwide, ensuring you test the real path of your internet traffic.

Wi-Fi & Cellular Comparative

Seamlessly switch and contrast fiber Wi-Fi versus 4G/5G mobile data latency, carrier routing efficiency, and packet loss.

Diagnostic Log Export
